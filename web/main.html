01 · What changes energy consumption

Pearson correlation · click a driver for interpretation
Driver −10+1 r
Duration is the dominant signal

Longer operational missions consume more energy (r = +0.814). This relationship is stronger than distance alone.

  • Payload effect: mean energy rises from 19.15 Wh at 0 g to 23.08 Wh at 500 g; partial r = +0.590 after controlling for duration.
  • Cohort reversal: wind, speed and acceleration variability change direction when 13 non-mission records are included.
  • Interpretation: correlation describes association, not an isolated causal effect.
